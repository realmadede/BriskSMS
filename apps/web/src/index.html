From customer payment to business clarity.

When a customer pays your business, BriskSMS helps turn the payment notification into an organized record, making it easier to understand which invoices have been paid and which payments still need attention.

STAGE 01

A customer makes a payment

A customer pays your business using a supported payment service such as Vodacom M-Pesa, Mixx by Yas, Airtel Money, HaloPesa, or supported bank channels.

Your business receives a notification

Your connected business phone receives the payment notification. The BriskSMS Android relay app securely captures the transaction notification.

BriskSMS organizes the payment

BriskSMS organizes the payment information so your team can find and review it easily, normalizing provider reference, amount, and timestamp.

COMMERCIAL INDEPENDENCE

A separate service. A separate choice.

Bulk Messaging is independently available and billed. You can use BriskSMS Payment Intelligence without purchasing Bulk SMS credits, or use Bulk Messaging independently. Payment reconciliation will never require prepaid messaging credits.

Paired phones

Keeping a phone reliable
  • Keep it plugged in. A flat battery stops payments from being read.
  • Turn off battery optimisation for the BriskSMS app, so Android does not close it in the background.
  • Keep it where it gets a good mobile signal.
  • Pair a second phone if payments cannot wait while the first one is off.

We keep your data for 30 days after you ask. Sign in again in that time and everything is restored as you left it. After 30 days your name, email, customers, messages, keys and settings are erased from the web, and your paired phones are told to wipe themselves.

2. BriskSMS is not a bank

BriskSMS is an independent software automation platform and is not a bank, electronic money issuer, or payment service provider licensed by the Bank of Tanzania.

We never hold, move or settle money. Payments go directly from your customers to your own mobile money till or bank account, and you keep full custody and settlement authority over those accounts. BriskSMS only reads payment messages so it can update your records.

3. The service

BriskSMS has two products, each subscribed to and billed separately:

  • Payment Intelligence records payment messages from your connected Android phones, organizes them and matches them to your invoices.
  • Bulk Messaging sends SMS to your customers using prepaid credits.

You can use Payment Intelligence without buying any messaging credits.

  • You are responsible for everything done through your account, including by team members you invite. Owners and admins control who has access.

5. The Android relay app

  • Install the app only on phones your business owns or controls, and pair them only with your own account.
  • The app reads notifications from supported payment providers and banks only. You must not change it or use it to collect any other messages.
  • You can revoke a paired phone at any time from the console. We may revoke a phone that behaves abnormally to protect your account.

6. Payment reconciliation

BriskSMS records each notification as evidence and matches payments to invoices using fixed rules. When a match is uncertain, it is marked for review by your team instead of being settled automatically.

A notification is information from your provider, not a guarantee of settlement. BriskSMS can only record notifications it receives: a phone that is switched off, offline or missing its permissions, or a provider that changes its message format, can delay or prevent a record. Check your till or bank statement before releasing high-value goods.

7. Bulk messaging

  • Send messages only to people who have agreed to hear from you, and include a way to opt out of promotional messages.
  • Follow the Tanzania Communications Regulatory Authority (TCRA) rules. BriskSMS does not send promotional messages between 20:00 and 07:00 EAT.
  • A custom alphanumeric Sender ID needs registration documents for your business before it can be used.
  • Do not send unlawful, misleading, abusive or fraudulent content.

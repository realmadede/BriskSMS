1. Who we are

BriskSMS is business software operated by [Company legal name], a company registered in the United Republic of Tanzania ([BRELA registration number]), with its registered office at [registered address].

This policy covers the BriskSMS website, the merchant console, the BriskSMS Relay Android app and the BriskSMS API. It explains what personal data we handle, why, and the choices you have under the Personal Data Protection Act No. 5 of 2022 (PDPA) and the Personal Data Protection Commission (PDPC) Regulations of 2023.

2. Our role

We handle two kinds of personal data, and our role is different for each:

  • Your account data. For the people who sign up and use BriskSMS, we decide how the data is used, so we act as the data controller.
  • Your customers' data. When a merchant uses BriskSMS to record payments or send messages, we process their customers' details on the merchant's instructions, so we act as a data processor and the merchant is the controller.

If you are a merchant's customer and have a question about a payment or message, contact that merchant first. We will help them answer you.

3. Data we collect

Account data

  • Your name, email address, organization name and, if you provide it, phone number.
  • Your password, which we store only as a one-way hash, never in readable form.
  • If you sign in with Google, the email address and name Google shares with us.

Payment message data (Payment Intelligence)

When a merchant connects an Android phone, the BriskSMS Relay app records the payment text messages (SMS) that supported mobile money providers and banks send to that phone. We keep the original message as evidence, and from it the payer's phone number and name, the transaction reference, the amount and the time.

App permissions

The BriskSMS Relay app asks for each permission only for the purpose below, and explains it first.

  • Receive SMS (direct-download version only): to receive payment messages from supported mobile money and bank senders. Any other message is dropped on the phone, unread and unstored. The app does not request permission to read your message history.
  • Notification access: to read the alert your Messages app shows for a payment message, in the version without the Receive SMS permission and as a back-up in the other. Alerts that are not payment messages are ignored.
  • Send SMS: to send the messages you queue in the console, through the phone's own SIM.
  • Phone state: to find the SIM cards in the phone so a message can go out on the one you choose.
  • Camera: only to scan the pairing QR code.
  • Notifications and unrestricted battery use: to show the ongoing relay notice and to keep the app running on an unattended phone.

Messaging data (Bulk SMS)

Recipient phone numbers and names you upload, message text, and delivery status.

Device data

For each paired phone: model, manufacturer, Android and app version, battery level and network type, and the phone's public security key.

Technical data

IP addresses and request details, used to keep the service secure and to limit repeated sign-in attempts.

4. Personal messages

The BriskSMS Relay app only keeps payment text messages from supported mobile money providers and banks, identified by their sender name. It ignores every other message and notification, including personal SMS, one-time codes, chats and email, and never stores them or sends them to our servers. It cannot read your message history.

5. How we use data

  • To provide BriskSMS: recording payments, matching them to invoices, and sending the messages you schedule.
  • To keep accounts and payments safe: detecting duplicates, preventing misuse and investigating security events.
  • To send service emails, such as email verification and password resets.
  • To meet our legal obligations.

Payment matching uses fixed, deterministic rules. When a match is uncertain, BriskSMS marks it for a person on your team to review. We do not use automated systems to make final settlement decisions.

We do not sell personal data and we do not use it for advertising.

6. Who we share it with

We share personal data only with providers that help us run BriskSMS, and only as far as they need it:

  • Hosting and database: [hosting provider and data location].
  • Email delivery: Resend, for verification and password-reset emails.
  • Google: only if you choose to sign in with Google.
  • SMS delivery: our SMS gateway and the mobile network operators that deliver your messages.
  • Integrations you connect: any third-party service you connect, such as an automation tool, a billing or access system, or your own webhook endpoint, receives the data you choose to send it.
  • Authorities: when the law requires it.

The website loads its fonts from Google Fonts, which means your browser shares your IP address with Google when you open a page.

7. How we protect it

  • All traffic between the apps, the website and our servers is encrypted with HTTPS.
  • Passwords are stored as one-way hashes. Session, API and device tokens are stored only as hashes.
  • Secrets for your integrations are encrypted with AES-256-GCM.
  • Each paired phone proves its identity with a key held in the Android Keystore, and you can revoke a phone at any time.
  • Every organization's data is kept separate, and sensitive actions are written to an append-only audit log.

8. How long we keep it

Recorded payment messages are kept as an unalterable financial record for as long as your account is active, plus [retention period after closure]. Account data is kept while your account is open and deleted [deletion period] after you close it, unless the law requires us to keep it longer.

Signed-in sessions expire after 30 days without use.

9. Your rights

Under the PDPA you can ask us to:

  • tell you what personal data we hold about you and give you a copy;
  • correct data that is wrong or incomplete;
  • delete data we no longer need, except records the law requires us to keep;
  • stop or limit certain uses of your data.

Email enterprise@brisksms.app to make a request. If you are unhappy with our answer, you can complain to the Personal Data Protection Commission.

10. Cookies

We use only the cookies needed to keep you signed in and protect your account. We do not use analytics, advertising or tracking cookies.

  • bsk_session keeps you signed in.
  • bsk_refresh renews your session for up to 30 days.
  • bsk_csrf protects forms against cross-site request forgery.

11. Data breaches

If a security incident affects personal data, we will notify the Personal Data Protection Commission within 72 hours of becoming aware of it, and tell affected merchants without delay so they can meet their own obligations.

Setup guide

From a new account to your first verified payment and first SMS

BriskSMS runs on an Android phone that stays at your business. The phone reads the payment text messages your mobile money and bank send you, and sends your SMS through its own SIM. This guide takes you from sign-up to both working.

What you need Steps How it works Troubleshooting Questions

What you need

  • An Android phone with a SIM

    It needs the SIM that receives your mobile money and bank payment messages, and it must be able to send SMS. An emulator cannot send real SMS, so use a physical phone. Keep it charged and connected to the internet.

  • A BriskSMS workspace

    Your workspace holds your payments, customers, invoices and paired phones. You create it when you register, and confirm your email address.

  • The phone you will leave on

    Pair the phone that stays at the till or the office. If payments cannot wait while it is off, pair a second phone as a backup.

Steps

  1. Create your workspace

    Choose Get started and register with your name, business name, email and a password of at least 8 characters. Open the verification link we email you, then sign in to the merchant console.

  2. Install the BriskSMS relay app

    Install the app on the phone from the previous section. The first time it opens, it shows a short introduction and then asks for the permissions it needs. Step 4 explains each one.

    Direct download

    Not on Google Play yet, or want the version that receives payment texts straight from the network? Download the APK to the phone and open it. Android asks you to allow installs from your browser the first time.

  3. Pair the phone with your workspace

    In the console, open Device fleet and choose Pair a phone. The console shows a QR code and a pairing code. In the app, scan the QR code, or choose to type the pairing code. The code works once and expires after 5 minutes. If it expires, generate a new one.

    Back in the console, choose Check connected devices. The phone appears under Paired phones with the status Active.

  4. Allow what the phone needs

    Android asks for these, and the app explains why. Notification access is turned on by you, in Android settings, because Android does not let any app grant it. The app opens that screen for you.

    Notification access
    Reads the alert your Messages app shows for a payment text from a supported mobile money or bank sender. Everything else is ignored and never stored.
    Receive SMS
    Direct-download version only. Receives payment texts from supported senders straight from the network, even when Messages notifications are off. Texts from any other sender are dropped on the phone, unread and unstored.
    Send SMS
    Lets the phone send your messages through its SIM.
    Phone state
    Finds your SIM cards, so a message can go out on SIM 1 or SIM 2.
    Notifications
    Shows the ongoing notice that the phone is relaying.
    Unrestricted battery use
    Stops Android from putting the app to sleep while the phone sits unattended.

  5. Check your first payment

    Receive a mobile money payment on the paired phone, or send a small one to yourself. The payment text arrives and BriskSMS reads it, so the payment appears in the Reconciliation feed. BriskSMS matches it to an invoice by its reference, or by the sender's number and exact amount. Anything uncertain goes to the Review queue for you to decide. A payment is never settled on a guess.

  6. Send your first SMS

    Open Cellular bulk SMS, enter your own number and a short message, and send. Bulk messaging needs a plan that includes it and prepaid message credits. The console tells you when either is missing. Messages leave from your own SIM, so customers see your number.

  7. Connect your own systems

    To send from your own software, create an API key and call the REST API. Use webhooks to receive verified payments, or connect spreadsheets through Zapier. The developer portal has the quickstart and every endpoint.

How it works

Payments

  1. Your mobile money or bank sends a payment text to the phone, and the phone reads it.
  2. The raw message is stored unchanged and sent to BriskSMS over an encrypted connection.
  3. BriskSMS reads the amount, sender and reference, and ignores duplicates.
  4. It matches the payment to an invoice, or sends it to your review queue.
  5. Your team verifies it. Only then is the invoice marked paid.

Messaging

  1. You send a message from the console or the API.
  2. BriskSMS checks your plan and your credits.
  3. The paired phone collects the job and sends it through the SIM you chose.
  4. The phone reports sent and delivered back to BriskSMS.
  5. Credits are deducted as messages are sent.

BriskSMS never holds your customers' money. Payments go from your customer to your own account, and BriskSMS only records the evidence.

Troubleshooting

The pairing code was refused
Each code works once and lasts 5 minutes. Choose Pair a phone again to get a new one, and enter it exactly as shown.
The phone shows Not responding
Open the app and check that the phone has internet, that every permission is on, and that battery use is unrestricted. Then keep the phone charging.
Payments do not appear
Check that the payment text reaches this phone: it must arrive in the Messages app on the paired SIM. Then check that notification access is on for BriskSMS in Android settings, and that Messages notifications are not switched off. The direct-download version also needs Receive SMS allowed. Texts from senders BriskSMS does not support are ignored on purpose.
A message was not sent
Check that your plan includes bulk messaging, that you have credits, and that the SIM you chose is in the phone. If the chosen SIM slot is empty, the phone uses the active SIM.
A payment is in the review queue
That is expected when the reference is missing, the amount is different, or more than one invoice could match. Approve it against the right invoice, or reject it.

Questions

Does BriskSMS hold my money?

No. BriskSMS is not a bank, wallet or payment processor. Your customers pay your own accounts, and BriskSMS records the evidence and matches it to your invoices.

Does the app read my personal messages?

No. It only keeps payment texts from supported mobile money and bank senders. Every other message is ignored on the phone and is never uploaded or stored.

Which networks and SIM cards work?

The app detects the SIM cards in the phone and handles Vodacom, Mixx by Yas, Airtel, Halotel and TTCL. On a dual-SIM phone you choose which SIM sends.

Can I use payments without buying message credits?

Yes. Payment reconciliation and bulk messaging are separate products with separate billing. Reconciliation never needs message credits.

What happens if the phone is offline?

The phone keeps what it has received and sends it when it is back online, and BriskSMS guards against sending the same message twice. Pair a second phone if payments cannot wait.
